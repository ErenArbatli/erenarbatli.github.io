Our running question

How does an immigration inflow affect local workers' wages?

Start with Card's study of the 1980 Mariel boatlift: a sudden arrival of Cuban migrants in Miami. Later studies give us connections to explore, not a ready-made verdict.

Start small. Keep the trail.

Bring a one-sentence question and, if you have them, one or two relevant papers. A broad proposal is enough to begin.

Use Zotero alongside the other guides. Discovery can begin with an explicit search or a question in Elicit; citation exploration adds another route. You do not need to run every search everywhere.

Examples are curated teaching illustrations, not live tool results. Your results will differ. Allow roughly 15–20 minutes per first attempt; installation may take longer.

Three reusable templates

  • Search log ↓ CSV

    Record the query, route, source link and reason for keeping a paper.

  • Reading note ↓ Markdown

    Separate what the authors say from your interpretation and next check.

  • Comparison matrix ↓ CSV

    Compare question, data/model, method, assumptions and finding, with evidence locations.

CSV files open in Excel or Google Sheets; the note is plain text.

Turn discovery into reading

  1. Select five candidates, not fifty.
  2. Identify two closest papers to read beyond the abstract.
  3. Fill the comparison from passages you have actually checked.
  4. Take one uncertain comparison to your supervisor.
A provisional contribution sentence

“The closest papers establish [specific finding in a defined setting]. I propose to examine [precise difference], which could change [economic interpretation]. I still need to verify [closest overlap or assumption].”

Fill this from checked comparisons, not from the absence of search results. A different country or dataset is not automatically a contribution.

Keep your judgement in charge

Discovery is not verification. A citation link does not establish agreement; a similar title does not establish the same question.

Label reading depth honestly: abstract only, selected sections or full paper. “Not yet checked” is better than a confident guess.

These pages do not run searches or receive your work. Do not upload confidential drafts, restricted data or colleagues' unpublished material to external services without permission and an appropriate policy check.
